Madeleine's Marine Animal Sanctuary

Madeleine's Marine Animal Sanctuary was founded by Madeleine Budgyan in 2020. Madeleine had always wanted to be a marine biologist and this was her dream. She worked hard, studied every day, and saved up money until she finally founded this amazing place. She has a Masters Degree in Marine Mammology from Berkely.

Her first recue came in late 2020, a baby dolphin that was sickly and dying. Madeleine and her small tem slowly nursed her back to health. The rest is history. We have saved over 200 animals and have had over 400 guests that all loved it.

Here at the sanctuary, we work to be as quick as possible with our cures while also giving our rescues the best treatment possible. We have been using loving and gentle treatment since day 1 and we plan to continue that way.
