The Toddler Experience

This experience is for ages 3-5. Parents are reccomended to come along but not required. In this one, we will mostly focus on planting your child's love for marine biology by playing fun games and letting them interact with some limited animals. Sign-ups are open for next Saturday 2pm-4pm. children are 50 dollars each and adults are free.

The Hands On Experience

This experience is for ages 6-65 with a height requirement of at least 4 feet 2 inches and a weight requirement of 55 pounds. In this experience, guests will get to go into the areas with the animals and play with them to a healthy extent. It is for all ages and focuses mostly on seeing the animals, playing games with them, and watching them do tricks than learning about each species. At least one adult per child is required. Sign-ups are open for Saturday 12 pm to 3pm. Minors are 90 dollars each and adults are 100 dollars.
