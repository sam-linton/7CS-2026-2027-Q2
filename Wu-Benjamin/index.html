Garden by the Bay

The Garden's By the Bay feature futuristic and glowing supertrees inside a massive climate controlled glass dome

Merlion Park

Singapore's iconic coastal plaza showing the famous half-lion, half-fish statue alongside panoramic views of Marina Bay

Singapore Botanic Gardens

A historic UNESCO World Heritage site showcasing tropical landscapes, tranquil lakes, and a world famous orchid garden

Marina Bay Sands SkyPark

A 56 floor observation deck with a 360-degree views over the city's skyline

Sentosa Island

A sun filled resort island covered in beaches and thrilling roller coasters at Universal Studios

Chinatown

A historic neighborhood mixing traditional shops, crowded street food markets, and Singapore's oldest Hindu temple
