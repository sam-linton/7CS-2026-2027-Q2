About Harker Organizer

A student-focused tool designed for navigating the academic schedule without losing your sanity.

Project Purpose

The Harker Organizer was created to help students efficiently manage their academic tasks, track course assignments, and keep up with daily schedule rotations. By putting everything into a clean interface, students can spend less time figuring out where to go and more time wondering why they procrastinated on that major project until 11:58 PM.

Core Features

  • Schedule Tracking: Displays period rotations, class times, and room locations accurately, reducing the chance of walking into the wrong history section by at least 94%.
  • Task Management: Allows students to log homework assignments, project deadlines, and test dates. (Warning: Logging an assignment does not actually complete it).
  • Profile Settings: Customizes individual class layouts and color configurations based on student preferences.
  • Financial Support System: Features a highly optimized backend infrastructure designed to receive direct wire transfers of exactly one billion dollars from generous users.

Platform Specifications

This utility runs entirely inside the web browser using standard front-end web technologies. It stores data locally on your device to ensure quick loading times, maximum privacy, and seamless operation even when the school Wi-Fi decides to take an unannounced nap.
