Klara and the Sun

By Kazuo Ishiguro

Klara and the Sun is another masterpiece from Ishiguro, a Nobel Prize Laureate and Booker Prize Winner. He weaves a delicate story of love, life, and death in a magnificent installment that will soon be turned into a movie with Jenna Ortega, Natasha Leone, Amy Adams, and Steve Buscemi. The Odyssey (Graphic Novel)

By Gareth Hinds

The new graphic novel adaptation of Homer's epic poem and sequel to the Illiad has beautiful illustrations that seem like they were ripped straight from reality, and the author, Gareth Hinds, is now working on an adaptation of The Aeneid. (This was written by me and not copied)
